## What it's built with

- [Astro](https://astro.build). Every page is plain HTML, built ahead of time, with barely any JavaScript.
- Plain CSS, and no colour on purpose. Just black, white and a couple of greys.
- [IBM Plex Sans and Mono](https://www.ibm.com/plex/), self-hosted.
- The little line drawings are hand-written SVG, generated from a fixed seed so they look the same every time.

## Where it lives

GitHub Pages, for free. Every time I push a change it gets checked (formatting, lint, types, broken links), built and published automatically.

## Can I see the code?

Sure, it's public on [GitHub](https://github.com/madhvanb/personal-portfolio).
