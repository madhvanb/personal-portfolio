## The problem

Spot drones in real time, in airspace full of other stuff.

## What I did

First I built graph neural network models for detection and tracking. That got detection accuracy
up by 15%.

Then I made them fast. I pruned the model and reworked the data pipeline, and detection time dropped
by 40%. That's the difference between a model that works and one that can actually keep up with busy
airspace.
