## The problem

Detect and track drones in real time, in airspace that's busy with other things.

## What I did

- Built graph neural network models for drone detection and tracking. Detection accuracy went up
  15%.
- Then made them fast: pruned the model and reworked the data pipeline, cutting detection time by
  40%. That was the difference between a model that works and one that keeps up with busy
  airspace.
