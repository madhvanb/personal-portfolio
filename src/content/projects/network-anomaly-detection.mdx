## The problem

Most network traffic is boring, and that's a good thing. The job is to find the small bit that
isn't, without drowning the people who investigate incidents in false alarms.

## What I'm doing

- Building feature-extraction and analysis pipelines for network traffic.
- Training anomaly detection models that flag suspicious communication patterns.
- Building analytics that show _why_ something got flagged, so someone can actually act on the
  alert.

It's ongoing, so I can't say as much here as I'd like yet.
