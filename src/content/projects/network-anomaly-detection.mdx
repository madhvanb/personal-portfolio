## The problem

Most network traffic is ordinary. The job is to find the small part that isn't, without burying the
people who investigate incidents in false alarms.

## What I'm doing

- Building feature-extraction and analysis pipelines for network traffic.
- Training anomaly detection models that flag suspicious communication patterns.
- Building analytics components that show _why_ something was flagged, for monitoring and incident
  investigation.

This is ongoing work, so there's less to say publicly than I'd like.
