## What I did

From my second year of the B.Tech. until graduation I worked as a research assistant:

- CNN and tree-based models to predict lung cancer and tuberculosis.
- Audio classification, comparing MFCC and spectrogram features.
- Interdisciplinary projects across healthcare and chemistry, including measuring coffee (more on
  the coffee page).

It led to four publications, a published patent application and two university research awards.
