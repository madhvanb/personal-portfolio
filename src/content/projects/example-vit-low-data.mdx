## Question

Do ViTs need huge datasets, or mostly the right inductive bias from training tricks?

## Setup

Trained ViT-S/16 from scratch on a 10k-image subset, varying augmentation strength and adding a
distillation token from a small CNN teacher. The loss with distillation weight $\lambda$:

$$
\mathcal{L} = (1 - \lambda)\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}}(y, \hat{y}) + \lambda\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{KL}}(p_{\text{teacher}} \,\|\, p_{\text{student}})
$$

## Result

Distillation was worth more than any single augmentation, and the gains stacked.
