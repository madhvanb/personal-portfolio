## The problem

Language models are great at writing confident health articles, including confidently wrong ones.
So the tool had to stick to its sources and catch claims that weren't backed up.

## What I did

- Built a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline: web-scraped sources, an embedding index,
  and LLaMA writing the drafts.
- Ran every generated article through a misinformation check.
- Wrapped it all in a Streamlit app and put it on AWS: EC2 for the app, S3 for data, and Lambda for
  the scheduled generation jobs.
